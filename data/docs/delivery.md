# Home delivery

## Delivery area and fees
We deliver within 5 km of the store. Orders placed before 16:00 are delivered the same day. Delivery costs 2.50 EUR and is free for orders above 30 EUR.

## Longer distances
Addresses between 5 km and 15 km get next-day delivery for 5 EUR. We do not deliver beyond 15 km.

## Refrigerated products
Refrigerated products such as insulin are delivered the same day only and must be handed to an adult. They cannot be sent with next-day delivery.
