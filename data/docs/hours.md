# Opening hours

## Regular hours
The store is open Monday to Saturday from 8:30 to 21:00 and on Sunday from 10:00 to 14:00.

## Public holidays
On public holidays the main store is closed. The emergency counter is open from 10:00 to 14:00 for urgent needs.

## Emergency line
Outside opening hours you can call the emergency line on +49 681 000111 until 23:00 for urgent medicine needs.
