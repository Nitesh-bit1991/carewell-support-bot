# Products

## What we stock
We stock prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins and supplements, first aid supplies, baby care, personal care and medical devices such as blood pressure monitors, thermometers and glucose meters.

## Stock availability
We cannot show live stock levels online. Call or message us to check if an item is available. Items we do not have in stock can be special ordered in 1 to 2 business days.

## Products not currently available

If a product is not currently available in the store, customers can ask the store to arrange a special order. Special orders usually take 1–2 business days. Customers should contact the store by phone or WhatsApp to ask about ordering an unavailable product.
