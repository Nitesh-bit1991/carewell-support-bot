# Returns and exchanges

## Medicines
For safety reasons medicines cannot be returned once they have left the store. If we gave you the wrong item or it is damaged or expired, bring it back within 48 hours with your receipt and we will exchange it.

## Medical devices
Unopened medical devices such as thermometers and blood pressure monitors can be returned within 14 days with the receipt. Opened hygiene products cannot be returned.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 3 business days.
