# Ordering and prescriptions

## How to order
You can order by phone, by WhatsApp on +49 170 0000000, through the order form on our website, or in the store. We confirm every order by message within 30 minutes during opening hours.

## Prescription medicines
Prescription medicines need a valid prescription. Send a clear photo by WhatsApp or bring the original to the store. Our pharmacist checks every prescription before the medicine is handed out.

## Medicines without prescription
Over-the-counter products can be ordered without a prescription. Some products have a quantity limit per customer.
